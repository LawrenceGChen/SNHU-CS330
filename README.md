1) How do I approach designing software?
   - What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?
     
   My work on the project helped me to translate complex real-world objects into primitive 3D meshes and how to arrange them relative to each other within a coordinate system.

   - What design process did you follow for your project work?
     
   I utilized reference and coordinate planning to establish scale, proportions, and local coordinate origins for each object. I also designed the visual architecture hierarchically, with basic wireframe geometry as a foundation, texture mapping onto the faces, and layering multi-source lighting to build visual depth.

   - How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?

    This project required me to utilize top-down decomposition to break down the complex task of rendering a 3D scene into its composite parts to minimize technical debt during development.

2) How do I approach developing programs?
   - What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?

     One new development strategy I utilized was low-level GPU data management. By working directly with the primitive shapes and simple textures, I was able to optimize the memory transfer between the CPU and GPU.

   - How did iteration factor into your development?
   
   I was regularly utilizing iteration when developing my 3D scene. It took a lot of minute tweaking and trial and error to ensure each object composed of multiple basic 3D shapes looked natural when rendered together.

   - How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones, which led you to the project’s completion?

  Over the course of the milestones I was able to shift from writing monolithic classes and main methods to structured architecture and giving each piece of the software individual attention so that it could come together to build a composite scene at the end.
